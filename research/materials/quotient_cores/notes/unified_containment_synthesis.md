## Critical correction: Slice space L₁

The first slice space of T_{333} is NOT {X ⊗ I_3} (which was used incorrectly in analysis 
and the note). The correct slice space is:

**L₁ = span{B_{ab} : a,b ∈ {0,1,2}}** where (B_{ab})_{3b+k, 3k+a} = 1 for k=0,1,2.

This comes from: T_{E_{ab}} = ∑_k e_{bk} ⊗ e_{ka} = ∑_k e_{3b+k} e_{3k+a}^T.

Key difference: {X ⊗ I_3} has entries at (3a+c, 3b+c), while L₁ has entries at (3b+k, 3k+a).
These are the SAME only on the diagonal a=b; for off-diagonal elements they differ.

Verification: naive_27 contains L₁ (correct) but NOT {X ⊗ I_3} (incorrect target).
