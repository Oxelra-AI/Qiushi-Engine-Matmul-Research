# finite-field support execution: rep2/rep16 full-through-E11 probes and old-witness elimination

> Historical research record. Read with the [correction guide](../../../corrections.md); recorded conclusions are not new verification receipts.

The active lower-bound subproblem is still the Wang A-side necessary condition for a length-20 decomposition of \(T_{\langle3,3,3\rangle}\) over \(\mathbb F_2\), after fixing one rank-1 A-factor to \(E_{11}\). The unresolved fixed-\(E_{11}\) second-orbit representatives are 2, 10, and 16 for rank-1 counts \(1\le k\le17\). This ARTIFACT moved the computation from rep10 proof replay to the independent question for reps 2 and 16: whether fuller Wang A-side information itself excludes their surviving supports.

I wrote [wang_fixed_e11_fullthrough_cpsat.py](../code/wang_fixed_e11_fullthrough_cpsat.py) to separate fixed branch points from the prefix used to choose high-dimensional Wang rows. The earlier range solver only added high-dimensional rows containing the whole fixed pair, such as \((E_{11},2)\) or \((E_{11},16)\). The new script can fix the same branch but add all rows containing only \(E_{11}\) in dimensions 4 through 8. A regression run on rep10 with through-points `1,10`, dimensions 1 plus 4 and 6, and no rank-1 projection reproduced the known CP-SAT INFEASIBLE result in 45.15 s at 14,478 Wang rows, so the new script agrees with the old semantics in the pair-through case ([rep010_regression_pairthrough_dim1_46_noproj.json](../results/fullthrough/rep010_regression_pairthrough_dim1_46_noproj.json)).

Full-through-E11 probes for reps 2 and 16 did not decide the branches. With fixed points \((1,2)\) or \((1,16)\), all dimension 1--3 rows, all dimension 4--8 rows containing \(E_{11}\), rank-1 count between 1 and 17, and the rank-1 projection, both CP-SAT runs remained UNKNOWN after about 1206 s of solve time. The rep2 file is [rep002_fullthroughE11_dim123_t48_rank1proj.json](../results/fullthrough/rep002_fullthroughE11_dim123_t48_rank1proj.json) with 1,237,519 Wang rows and 149,444 rank-1 projected rows. The rep16 file is [rep016_fullthroughE11_dim123_t48_rank1proj.json](../results/fullthrough/rep016_fullthroughE11_dim123_t48_rank1proj.json) with 1,229,071 Wang rows and 38,756 projected rows after branch exclusions. Removing the rank-1 projection and shorter exact-count probes also remained UNKNOWN on the tested budgets (`rep002_fullthroughE11_dim123_t48_nor1proj_t300.json`, `rep016_fullthroughE11_dim123_t48_nor1proj_t300.json`, `rep002_exact17_fullthroughE11_dim123_t48_rank1proj.json`, `rep016_exact14_fullthroughE11_dim123_t48_rank1proj.json`). Thus full-through-E11 Wang information is materially stronger but has not yet become a proof or a surviving full-LUT support.

I corrected the local support scanner [classify_support_lut_violations.py](../../occupation_and_completion/code/classify_support_lut_violations.py). A first version scanned only subset spans of the support, which is not complete for raw Wang lower bounds because a larger subspace can carry a stronger lower bound than the span of its occupied points. The corrected default scans all 8,283,458 expanded Wang rows. Complete scans of the old pair-through witnesses confirmed that they are not full-table admissible. For the saved rep2 support in [e11_rep002_k01_17_dim123_throughfixed_rank1proj.json](../results/wang_fixed_e11_range_cpsat/rep002_k01_17/e11_rep002_k01_17_dim123_throughfixed_rank1proj.json), the full scan [rep002_saved_support_full_lut_classification_allviol.json](../../occupation_and_completion/results/fullthrough/rep002_saved_support_full_lut_classification_allviol.json) finds 246 violations, maximum excess 2, distributed as 120 rows containing \(E_{11}\), 60 containing point 2, and 66 containing neither. For the saved rep16 support in [e11_rep016_k01_17_dim123_throughfixed_rank1proj.json](../results/wang_fixed_e11_range_cpsat/rep016_k01_17/e11_rep016_k01_17_dim123_throughfixed_rank1proj.json), [rep016_saved_support_full_lut_classification_allviol.json](../../occupation_and_completion/results/fullthrough/rep016_saved_support_full_lut_classification_allviol.json) finds 555 violations, maximum excess 2, distributed as 125 rows containing \(E_{11}\), 78 containing point 16, and 352 containing neither. The containment split matters: full-through-E11 rows alone cut many old witnesses but not all of their violations, so a complete raw-LUT solve still needs rows not tied to the fixed prefix.

The old supports are nevertheless useful as objects to decompose. In rep2 the 20-point witness has 17 rank-1 points and three rank-2 points \(\{25,194,217\}\); all three rank-2 points lie in the pointwise-stabilizer orbit represented by 25. In rep16 the 20-point witness has 14 rank-1 points and six rank-2 points \(\{19,21,23,25,33,41\}\), split among third-orbit representatives 19, 20, and 33 under the stabilizer of \((1,16)\). I wrote [rank1_skeleton_with_branch.py](../../occupation_and_completion/code/rank1_skeleton_with_branch.py), which fixes a higher-rank prefix and asks whether any rank-1 skeleton of the required size can complete it while satisfying the complete Wang LUT, with the correct parent second-orbit branch exclusions.

The complete-LUT rank-1 skeleton tests killed both old high-rank prefixes quickly. For rep2, fixed rank-1 points \(\{1,2\}\), fixed high points \(\{25,194,217\}\), exact 17 rank-1 points, and the rep2 parent branch are INFEASIBLE after a 52.50 s compression and 2.72 s CP-SAT solve; see [rep002_high_25_194_217_branch_rank1skeleton.json](../results/fullthrough/rep002_high_25_194_217_branch_rank1skeleton.json). For rep16, fixed rank-1 points \(\{1,16\}\), fixed high points \(\{19,21,23,25,33,41\}\), exact 14 rank-1 points, and the rep16 parent branch are INFEASIBLE after a 53.49 s compression and 0.27 s solve; see [rep016_high_19_21_23_25_33_41_branch_rank1skeleton.json](../results/fullthrough/rep016_high_19_21_23_25_33_41_branch_rank1skeleton.json). These runs show that the old supports are not near-valid objects merely needing small rank-1 replacements; their high-rank skeletons are incompatible with the complete table. They do not exclude other high-rank prefixes in the same branches.

The rep10 proof-replay jobs from analysis also returned during this ARTIFACT and did not yield a certificate. The 5,572-row orbit-core CNF has 1,407,015 variables and 3,119,074 clauses and CaDiCaL timed out after 3600 s with no SAT/UNSAT; status path [rep10_orbit_core.status.json](../results/rep10_orbit_core_cnf/rep10_orbit_core.status.json). The 2,166-row lazy-core CNF has 270,699 variables and 681,588 clauses and also timed out after 3600 s; status path [rep10_lazy2166.status.json](../results/rep10_lazy2166_cnf/rep10_lazy2166.status.json). Z3 row-core extraction on the 5,572-row core returned `unknown` after 900 s in [z3_core_from_orbit_core.json](../results/rep10_core/z3_core_from_orbit_core.json). Therefore rep10 remains a CP-SAT-infeasible row family without an independently checked proof object.

The peer related analysis correction in record is incorporated: the D=I correction-pair budget from the earlier analysis should not be used as a lower-bound constraint, because the coefficient matrix is free enough to reuse one pivot UV rank-one matrix across derived columns. Their corrected canonical object is the span/quotient oracle for nine-dimensional UV-slice spaces. That is relevant if related analysis obtains a full A-side support or a quotient-plane candidate, but it does not change the finite-field Wang A-support state reached here.

The current scientific picture is sharper but still open. Full-through-E11 rows strongly affect reps 2 and 16, and old witnesses fail the complete table in structured ways; fixed high-prefix skeletons for those witnesses are impossible. However the full rep2 and rep16 fixed-second branches remain undecided by the tested CP-SAT models. The next useful execution is to decompose reps 2 and 16 by higher-rank prefix structure under the pointwise stabilizer and by rank-1 count, reusing the complete-LUT rank-1 skeleton compression so each high-prefix branch is a small 49-variable feasibility problem. If many high-prefix branches survive, those surviving A-supports should be scanned against the full LUT and then handed to a coupled A/B/C solve or related analysis's span oracle as appropriate; if high-prefix branches die systematically, the pattern may give a smaller proof-producing route than the current million-row full-through-E11 formula.
