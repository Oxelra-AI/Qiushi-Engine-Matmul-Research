## Exact kernel calculation

Using the BILR base triple `(A₀,B₀,C₀)` from the reconstructed `Z₄×Z₃` rank-23 scheme, the `sl₃`-restricted exterior Jacobian has shape `84×24` and ranks

- `rank_Q = 14`,
- `rank mod 65521 = 14`,
- `rank mod 32003 = 14`.

Thus the tangent kernel dimension is `24-14=10`.

The following ten infinitesimal group directions were then constructed explicitly:

1. **Eight internal recombination directions**
   \[
   (A,B,C)\mapsto (A,B,C)M,
   \qquad M\in SL_3.
   \]
   Infinitesimally these are right multiplications of the `9×3` matrix with columns `(A,B,C)` by the eight standard `sl₃` generators. Since
   \[
   (AM_1)\wedge(AM_2)\wedge(AM_3)=\det(M)(A\wedge B\wedge C),
   \]
   they preserve the exterior equation for `M∈SL₃`.

2. **Two nontrivial centralizer-conjugation directions** generated by `a₀` and `a₀²`:
   \[
   (A,B,C)\mapsto (hAh^{-1},hBh^{-1},hCh^{-1}),\quad h\in Z(a₀).
   \]
   The identity direction is zero in commutator form, so only two directions are nonzero in `PGL`.

The exact ranks are:

- rank of these ten symmetry columns over `Q`: `10`,
- rank modulo `65521`: `10`,
- rank of `J · symmetry_columns` modulo `65521`: `0`,
- rank of `symmetry_columns` together with a modular kernel basis: `10`.

Therefore the tangent kernel at BILR is exactly the tangent to this group orbit. Combined with analysis's vanishing second-order obstruction and rank-14 Jacobian, this supports the local statement: **the BILR single-free-`Z₄` exterior component is locally the group orbit of the BILR exterior triple**, with 8 effective internal parameters after quotienting by centralizer conjugation. This does not list or exclude other components of the rank-4 Cartan exterior fiber.

## Correction to the affine-linear reading

The analysis sentence "the exterior fiber is an affine linear subspace" is not used. The exact check shows the correct mechanism.

- For an internal tangent `N∈sl₃`, the finite move is `(A,B,C)(I+tN)` and the wedge is scaled by `det(I+tN)`. A linear ARTIFACT at `t=1` lies on the exterior fiber only when `det(I+N)=1`.
- The script records examples: a nilpotent `E01` has `det(I+N)=1` and preserves `ω`; diagonal examples with determinant `0` satisfy `S₄(A∧B∧C)=0`, not `ω`.
- Centralizer conjugation preserves the exterior equation as finite conjugation; the first-order linear truncation need not be an exact finite point.

Thus the apparent flatness of selected basis directions was a coordinate artifact, not a property of the fiber.

## Residual cubic anchor

For an internal `SL₃` recombination `M`, define
\[
(A_M,B_M,C_M)=(A_0,B_0,C_0)M.
\]
The corresponding symmetric residual cubic is
\[
C_M=\operatorname{tr}(X^3)-3\sum_{k=0}^3
(\operatorname{Ad}(a_0)^k A_M\cdot X)
(\operatorname{Ad}(a_0)^k B_M\cdot X)
(\operatorname{Ad}(a_0)^k C_M\cdot X).
\]
At `M=I`, the coefficient vector of `C_M` matches exactly the eleven BILR published cubes. The analysis Koszul--Young ranks for this residual are all `722` over primes `1000003`, `1000033`, `65521`, and `32003`; with the rank-one upper contribution `70`, this rules out a ten-cube expression only for the BILR residual itself.

A nontrivial unipotent internal move with determinant one preserves the exterior equation but changes 53 residual coefficients relative to the BILR cube residual, so the residual family is genuinely nonconstant along the internal `SL₃` orbit. Centralizer conjugation only makes a linear variable substitution in the residual and therefore preserves Waring rank; it should not be counted as a source of new rank-10 behavior.
