2. More importantly, its `F7`/`T2` UV action convention did not satisfy `g_E*T_flat = T_flat*h_W`; the run itself printed `False` for those two generators. The corrected scripts below use the finite convention paired with the already verified QMM covector action.

The retained analysis facts that survive after correction are the structural dimensions: for the order-7 diagonal sandwich on \(E/L\), the fixed dimension is 12 and the two nontrivial pieces have dimensions 30 and 30. analysis recomputes the needed fixed-space and orbit data with the preserving action and records an exact finite-family exclusion.

## Corrected finite sandwich action

The matrix-multiplication tensor is represented as an \(81\times 9\) flattening \(T_{\rm flat}\), with row-major UV coordinates. For a diagonal sandwich \(\phi_{F,F,F}\), this investigation convention acts on the first two covector factors by
\[
X\mapsto F^T X F^{-T},
\qquad
M=\operatorname{kron}(F^T,F^{-1})
\]
in row-major coordinates. The paired action on the nine W-slice coefficients is
\[
X\mapsto F^{-1}XF,
\qquad
h_W=\operatorname{kron}(F^{-1},F^T).
\]
For the order-7 matrix
\[
F=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&1\\1&0&1\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix},
\]
the script verifies exactly over \(\mathbb F_2\):

- `gE_T_equals_T_hW = true`, where \(g_E=M\otimes M\);
- `gbar_Q_equals_Q_gE = true` for the induced action on \(E/L\);
- \(F,M,g_E,h_W,\bar g\) all have order 7.

The replay file is [order7_family_certificate_v2.json](../results/order7_exhaustive/order7_family_certificate_v2.json); the producing script is [order7_family_certificate_v2.py](../code/order7_family_certificate_v2.py).

## Exact orbit and fixed-space data

The verified data are:

- \(\dim \operatorname{Fix}_E(g_E)=15\);
- \(L_0=L\cap \operatorname{Fix}_E(g_E)\) has dimension 3, and the script checks this equality directly;
- \(\dim \operatorname{Fix}(E/L)=12\);
- \(\sigma=I+g_E+\cdots+g_E^6\) has rank 15, is idempotent, and has image equal to \(\operatorname{Fix}_E(g_E)\);
- all \((2^9-1)^2=261121\) nonzero rank-one UV points are enumerated;
- under \(\langle g_E\rangle\), there are 49 fixed rank-one points and 37296 seven-orbits;
- the 49 fixed rank-one points span a 9-dimensional space \(B\), with \(B\cap L=0\) and hence \(B\cap L_0=0\).

The free-orbit profile by orbit span rank and orbit-sum location is:

| orbit span rank | orbit-sum location | count |
|---:|---|---:|
| 3 | \(L_0\), actually zero | 28 |
| 4 | \(p+L_0\), actually a fixed rank-one point \(p\) | 196 |
| 6 | \(L_0\), actually zero | 308 |
| 7 | other fixed vector | 35392 |
| 7 | \(p+L_0\), actually a fixed rank-one point \(p\) | 1372 |

Equivalently:

- no free orbit has a nonzero orbit sum inside \(L_0\);
- for every fixed rank-one point \(p\), every free orbit whose sum lies in \(L_0+\langle p\rangle\) has sum exactly \(p\), never \(p+\ell\) with \(0\ne\ell\in L_0\);
- for each of the 49 possible \(p\), the filtered orbit count is 32, split as 28 rank-7 orbits and 4 lower-rank orbits, all with orbit sum \(p\).

## Finite-family theorem over \(\mathbb F_2\)

Let \(S\subset E\) be spanned by at most 22 nonzero rank-one UV points, and suppose these points are permuted by the diagonal order-7 sandwich subgroup above. Then \(S\) is a union of fixed points and seven-orbits. If \(L\subseteq S\), write \(P\) for the span of the chosen fixed rank-one points and let \(m\) be the number of selected seven-orbits. Since \(L_0\subset L\subset S\) and \(P\subset S\cap \operatorname{Fix}(g_E)\), while \(B\cap L_0=0\),
\[
P\oplus L_0\subseteq S\cap\operatorname{Fix}(g_E)=\sigma(S).
\]
On the other hand, \(\sigma(S)\) is generated by \(P\) together with the \(m\) orbit sums, so
\[
\dim P+3\le \dim \sigma(S)\le \dim P+m.
\]
Thus \(m\ge 3\).

For rank \(\le 20\), three seven-orbits already use 21 terms, impossible. For rank 21, one must have exactly three seven-orbits and no fixed point; then \(\sigma(S)\) would have to contain \(L_0\), but the enumeration shows every orbit sum lying in \(L_0\) is zero. For rank 22, one has exactly three seven-orbits and one fixed point \(p\); the fixed-part dimension is tight, so each orbit sum must lie in \(L_0+\langle p\rangle\), but the enumeration shows all such sums equal \(p\), so \(\sigma(S)=\langle p\rangle\) cannot contain \(L_0\).

Therefore no such termwise diagonal order-7 sandwich-symmetric rank-\(\le 22\) UV-span representation exists over \(\mathbb F_2\). By the canonical span formulation, this excludes every rank-\(\le22\) decomposition over \(\mathbb F_2\) whose rank-one UV terms are permuted by this diagonal order-7 sandwich subgroup.

The script also checks that \(GL_3(\mathbb F_2)\) has 168 elements, 48 order-7 elements, and 8 order-7 subgroups, all conjugate. Hence the same statement holds for every diagonal order-7 sandwich subgroup over \(\mathbb F_2\) after conjugating the chosen Singer matrix.

## What this does and does not address

This is a real finite-family exclusion, but it is not a global proof that \(R_{\mathbb F_2}(T_{333})\ge 23\). It treats termwise permutation by a diagonal order-7 sandwich subgroup. It does not address a merely invariant 22-dimensional UV subspace whose selected rank-one generators are not themselves a union of group orbits. It also does not transfer to characteristic zero without a separate algebraic argument, and it does not cover non-diagonal De Groote triples \(\phi_{U,V,W}\) with \(U,V,W\) not simultaneously diagonal in the same sandwich parameter.

## Relation to Yang's symmetric-search literature

The local Yang source `Knowledge/objects/papers/Ruling-Out-Low-rank-Matrix-Multiplication-Tensor-Decompositions-with-Sym--67780cbdf115--0a17695103fc/object.md` records the relevant context:

- lines 51--53: the 2024 SAT work searches over \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\) and restricts to rank \(\le 21\) because \(\log_3 21<\log_2 7<\log_3 22\);
- lines 65--81: De Groote symmetries include the sandwich action \(\phi_{U,V,W}(A,B,C)=(UAV^{-1},VBW^{-1},WCU^{-1})\), and termwise symmetry means the decomposition is preserved up to permutation of triplets;
- lines 87--97: Yang's earlier two works searched single-generator and cyclic-augmented symmetry groups up to rank 23, found some rank-23 examples, and left many \(\langle f\rangle\) groups open for rank 21;
- lines 101--105: the 2024 result excludes rank \(\le21\) for certain cyclic+transpose and cyclic+sandwich groups, while pure cyclic at rank 16 remained computationally hard.

Our analysis result is narrower in symmetry type but sharper at rank 22 for a named diagonal order-7 sandwich family, and its proof is a replayable four-second rank-one-orbit certificate rather than a large SAT instance.

## Corrected order-3 setup (not yet a theorem)

The companion script [order3_profile.py](../code/order3_profile.py) applies the same corrected finite sandwich convention to the diagonal order-3 cyclic-permutation matrix
\[
F_3=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\0&0&1\\1&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
It verifies `gE_T_equals_T_hW = true` and `gbar_Q_equals_Q_gE = true`. The profile file is [order3_profile.json](../results/order3_profile/order3_profile.json).

Recorded setup facts:

- \(\dim\operatorname{Fix}_E=27\), \(\dim L_0=3\), and \(\dim\operatorname{Fix}(E/L)=24\);
- the rank-one points split as 49 fixed points and 87024 three-orbits;
- the fixed rank-one span again has dimension 9 and zero intersection with \(L\) and \(L_0\);
- possible full-UV-rank 22 skeletons under termwise order-3 permutation are \((m,f)=(5,7),(6,4),(7,1)\) for three-orbits and fixed points, respectively;
- free orbit profile: 294 lower-rank orbits have zero sum in \(L_0\), one rank-3 orbit has a nonzero sum in \(L_0\), 84644 rank-3 orbits have other fixed sums, and 2085 rank-3 orbits have sums in some \(p+L_0\) (2058 exactly fixed-rank-one sums, 27 with a nonzero \(L_0\) component).

This order-3 data is only a setup. The fixed-part budget is weaker than in the order-7 case, and no rank-22 exclusion has been proved for order-3 in this ARTIFACT.
